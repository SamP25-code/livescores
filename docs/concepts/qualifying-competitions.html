How it fits together

  1. Turn on "Entry by qualification" in a competition's settings and it gets a Qualifying tab (Super and that competition's admins).
  2. Each place has a source: a qualifying competition's winner, or a wildcard. The list doubles as the public "How to qualify" page - advertising those competitions.
  3. Qualifying competition on this site (e.g. the October Singles): pick it - its champion fills the place automatically when its final finishes.
  4. Not on this site: add its name, where, date of the final and how much to record (final only, semis, quarters, or just the winner). Results go in with the person search; the winner fills the place. It also becomes a small results page of its own.
  5. Duplicates are flagged, not decided - "Already qualified via place 9 - make this a wildcard?" - and a wildcard keeps its original source on record.
