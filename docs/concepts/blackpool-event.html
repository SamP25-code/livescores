Alan Hart wins the Fylde Classic and books his Masters place

Saturday 5th June 2027

Alan Hart beat Colin Ashworth 21-17 in a tense final at Kirkham to win the Fylde Classic - and with it, a place in this year's Masters Invitation 32.

It's Alan's first Masters since 2023. He joins Tim Hodge as the second qualifier from the Fylde coast.
